## Before you post
1. **Link the product:** add the product link in TikTok Shop (the yellow basket) so the "Tekan Beg Kuning" CTA works.
2. **Music:** the video already has an original, royalty-free score. To ride a trend, add a sound from the
   **Commercial Music Library** (affiliate/business accounts can only use that library), then set
   *Original sound* to about 30–50% so the impacts still hit on the cuts.
3. **Disclosure:** turn on the *Content disclosure → Promotional content / Paid partnership* toggle where your account requires it.
4. **Claims:** the on-screen specs (13mm, BT 6.0, ENC, 30ms, IPX5, 1-year warranty, COD) come from the seller's listing.
   Check that the listing you link still states them.
